About us

A wallet for what you own

Mulk (ملك — "possession") is building the GCC's possession wallet. We started with the region's most valuable asset — real estate — giving owners live valuations across all six Gulf states, drawn from official land-department and statistics data.

But not everyone owns a building. Many people's most treasured possessions are their watch, their car, or a prized plate number. So Mulk turns each of those into an interactive, valued 3D piece you can keep in the same wallet — captured from your own belongings in seconds.

Methodology

How Mulk values what you own

Every asset is valued on the same disciplined basis — its real, observed market value, never a guess. Our approach follows the internationally recognised valuation principles that underpin the WTO Customs Valuation framework: fair, uniform and neutral, and grounded first in an asset's actual transaction value.

◈ Aligned with WTO Customs Valuation principles · Article VII, GATT 1994
We value on actual market transactions first. Where a direct transaction isn't available, we step — in a defined order — to comparable sales, then to market-derived estimates, exactly as the international standard prescribes. Nothing is arbitrary or fictitious.

The valuation hierarchy

Applied in order — each step is used only when the one above it isn't available.

1
Transaction value
The actual price paid in a real, arm's-length sale — the primary basis for every valuation.
2
Identical assets
The transaction value of the same asset — matched on model, reference, area or specification.
3
Similar assets
Closely comparable assets that are commercially interchangeable with the one being valued.
4
Deductive value
Worked back from resale prices, adjusted for the costs and margins between markets.
5
Computed value
Built up from replacement or retail cost plus the premium the market pays.
6
Reasoned estimate
For rare or one-of-a-kind assets, a reasoned estimate consistent with all of the above and the best available data.

Two sides of every market

◔ Primary market

New-issue and first-sale activity — the values at which assets first enter the market.

◕ Secondary market

Resale, auction and private-sale activity — what assets actually change hands for today.

Mulk blends both sides continuously, so a valuation reflects the living market — not just list prices. Figures refresh every day.

Every class is re-valued automatically each morning, drawing on a broad range of primary and secondary market data across the GCC and beyond.

Mulk valuations are informational estimates derived from market data, not formal appraisals. Rare and unique assets can vary widely — owners can flag any figure for review.
