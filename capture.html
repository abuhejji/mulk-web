For a clean scan
  • Put it on a clean, empty surface — nothing else in frame.
  • Don't hold it: a hand hides the surface we need to see.
  • Soft bags slouch — lightly stuff it so it keeps its shape. Leaning is fine.
  • Rotate it slowly on the spot (a turntable / lazy-Susan is perfect), or move around it.
  • Even, soft light. Avoid glare on hardware, leather sheen or glass.

Your camera runs on your device. Frames are processed locally to remove the background; nothing is uploaded by this prototype. Building the final 3D model is a separate step.
