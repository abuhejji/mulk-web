Scan your asset in 3D

Slowly move your phone around the item — Mulk captures it from every angle and isolates it from everything else.

For a clean scan
  • Put the item on a plain surface — don't hold it (a hand covers what we need to see).
  • Even, soft light. Avoid harsh glare on metal or glass.
  • Move the camera slowly in a full circle, then a little higher and lower.

Your camera runs on your device. Frames are processed locally to remove the background; nothing is uploaded by this prototype. Building the final 3D model is a separate step.
